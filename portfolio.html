01 — Methodology

How I Build Each Forecast

⚡ Price Structure
LMP Decomposition
Each forecast splits the settlement point price into λ energy and congestion. System lambda is forecast once per hour slot statewide; congestion is forecast per hub based on local topology and known outages.
🗺 Coverage
Five ERCOT Hubs
HB_NORTH Dallas · HB_HOUSTON · HB_SOUTH San Antonio · HB_WEST Midland · HB_PAN Panhandle — at 4 daily hour slots: 7 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM, 10 PM CPT.
☀️ Process
Morning Thesis First
Wind generation outlook, demand forecast, temperature drivers, and known transmission outages are documented before any prices are entered. Reasoning lives separately from the numbers.
📡 Data
ERCOT CDR Reports
DAM and RT settlement prices are pulled from ERCOT's CDR HTML reports via a Node.js sync tool. The Interval Ending :00 row is used as the HE representative price — matching ERCOT's dashboard display.
📐 Scoring
DAM-Relative Accuracy
Score = 60% delta-based (anchored at $50/MWh) + 40% vs-DAM. Beating the DAM earns a bonus. The benchmark isn't perfection — it's whether I'm adding signal beyond what the market already priced in.
🌙 Review
Evening Post-Mortem
After RT settles, each day gets a written review: what drove the market, where the thesis was right or wrong, and what would have changed the call. Congestion accuracy is tracked separately from energy.
