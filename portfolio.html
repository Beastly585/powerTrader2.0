Methodology

⚡
Price Structure
LMP Decomposition
Each forecast decomposes settlement point price into λ (energy + losses) and congestion. System lambda is forecast once per hour slot and applied statewide; congestion is forecast per hub based on local topology and outage conditions.
🗺
Coverage
Five ERCOT Hubs
Forecasts cover HB_NORTH (Dallas), HB_HOUSTON, HB_SOUTH (San Antonio), HB_WEST (Midland), and HB_PAN (Panhandle) at four daily hour slots: 7 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM, and 10 PM CPT.
☀️
Process
Morning Thesis First
Each day begins with a written market thesis — wind outlook, demand forecast, temperature drivers, transmission outages — before any prices are entered. Reasoning is documented separately from the numbers.
📡
Data Sources
ERCOT CDR Reports
DAM and RT settlement prices are pulled from ERCOT's CDR HTML reports via a local Node.js sync tool. The Interval Ending :00 value is used as the HE representative price, matching ERCOT's dashboard display.
📐
Scoring
DAM-Relative Accuracy
Score = 60% delta-based (anchored at $50/MWh reference) + 40% vs-DAM component. Beating the DAM directionally earns a bonus. Matching DAM scores ~50. Doing worse than DAM scores lower. Clamped 0–100.
🌙
Review
Evening Post-Mortem
After RT settles, each day gets an evening review: what drove the market, where the thesis was right or wrong, what would have changed the call. Congestion accuracy tracked separately from energy.
