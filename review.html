⚡ Spike Day Note

Percentage accuracy can be misleading on spike days. On a $200/MWh spike, being off by $30 shows as 85% — but that's a large miss in $ terms.

Always check MAE ($/MWh) alongside percentage accuracy, especially on days tagged high-price or congestion.
